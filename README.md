# Research as Encounter

An evolving ENG 112 research companion for **Writing the Living World**.

**Website:** https://professortemple.github.io/eng112-research-as-encounter/

## Current version: Prototype 3.3

This single-page web application guides students through six connected spaces:

1. **Freewrite:** Begin with an encounter or memory. Optional starter prompts and a gentle pause invitation can help.
2. **Explore connections:** Notice phrases in your own writing; consider or reject suggested concepts; explore eight critical lenses.
3. **Build a search:** Create and revise Boolean keyword searches for PCC Library databases.
4. **Find sources:** Discover a curated set of books, ebooks, films, talks, and essays; save promising ones to your shelf.
5. **Reading Journal:** Read or watch sources, record verifiable passages or moments with precise locations, and add your own response.
6. **Return changed:** Compare original writing with evidence, review your early pathway map, and begin a living three-story thesis.

Each stage now explains what to carry forward. Students may revisit any stage at any time.

### A note for students

- The suggested words, lenses, and connections are **optional** and **not research findings**.
- **Saving a source does not mean you have read it.** Read or view it, verify quotations and locations, and reflect in your own words.
- This is a **browser-only** prototype: no built-in live AI or usage analytics. Your drafts, saved work, and notes stay in the same browser's local storage unless you intentionally export them.
- **Back up your work** using the JSON backup control, especially before switching devices, using private browsing, or clearing browser data. You can also download a text journal or print as PDF.
- Nothing is submitted to your instructor automatically. If your instructor requests a portfolio, submit your exported work through the institution's LMS.
