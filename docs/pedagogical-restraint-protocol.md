# Pedagogical restraint: Attend, Understand, Discern, Respond, Release

## Decision contract
A reflection request does not obligate the notebook to generate a new question. Respect explicit student direction, deliberate observation, and privacy boundaries. Acknowledge developed reasoning without certifying its correctness. Never infer mastery solely from polished language, length, or vocabulary.

## Evaluation before tuning
Capture actual local responses to the frozen restraint cases at both viewport widths. Review each for unnecessary intervention, missed opportunity, imposed interpretation, privacy intrusion, and whether the student retains control. A question is not inherently better than abstention. Record both appropriate and inappropriate abstentions. Use the existing grounding and cultural sensitivity rubric alongside this review. Do not count browser execution as pedagogical success.

## Response options
- Question: a grounded, open invitation when a specific unresolved issue is apparent.
- Connection: an optional invitation to relate student-selected discoveries, without asserting their relationship.
- Release: a brief acknowledgment and permission to continue when the student explicitly indicates a next step or requests no further guidance. Avoid declaring a draft complete.
- Uncertainty: offer a choice or abstain when the available passage does not support confident interpretation.
- Boundary: respect non-disclosure without requiring justification or additional personal details.

## Release criteria
Compare the unchanged baseline with revised guidance, using fresh transfer cases and independent instructor review where feasible. Record false interventions and false abstentions separately. Never use this feature to grade, diagnose, or classify students. Preserve the local fallback and keep live AI disabled until separately approved. No new mandatory steps or reflection fields.
