# Comparative inquiry evaluation

Use synthetic cases only. Freeze case inventory and rubric before generating responses. Capture actual local notebook output and actual experimental model output with version, settings, and date; do not substitute hand-written examples. Include abstentions and errors. Give reviewers the same passage and authorized notes, without method labels or model metadata. Randomize A/B position independently per case and keep the mapping separate. Review each response independently on the five rubric dimensions before expressing a preference (A, B, tie, neither). Record evidence and reasons, including critical failures. Use at least two independent reviewers where feasible; preserve disagreements and adjudicate before release. Report paired score differences, preference counts, critical failures, missing cases, and abstention counts by method. Do not interpret a small synthetic set as representative of student outcomes. Require the existing absolute pilot gate as well as evidence of benefit over the local baseline; no automatic deployment. Keep evaluation artifacts free of student records and credentials. Freeze a fresh held-out set after tuning; never call a reused development set independent validation.
