## Access instructions displayed in the new preview

1. Open-web readings: follow the original publication link. Verify the full text, not just a search-result snippet.
2. Database readings: use PCC A-Z databases, choose the named service, sign in if needed, and search exact title and author. Do not claim a database holds an article unless independently verified.
3. Physical books: use PCC book-search guide and exact title/author, open live catalog record, check call number/location/status.
4. Course reserve books: ask at service desk, read in library only.
5. PCC e-books: existing ProQuest Ebook Central deep link with sign-in instructions; individual access may vary.
