# Pedagogy
Research as Encounter: intellectual memory, curiosity, discovery, courage, responsibility, agency and reciprocity. Preserve student voice and authorship; never generate essay paragraphs. Invite rather than prescribe interpretations. Distinguish observation, interpretation and significance; do not equate three stories with three paragraphs. Support sensory detail, lived experience, source dialogue, alternative explanations, more-than-human relationships and revision. Keep four familiar spaces: Discover, Research, Thesis, Write. Vocabulary travels through search, reading, thesis and essay; related terms are not necessarily synonyms. ENG 112 requires abstract and keywords; other requirements follow the actual assignment. Local private writing by default; no new telemetry without review and informed consent. Every action should show where the student is, what happened and a clear next move.
